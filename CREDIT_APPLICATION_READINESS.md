# Startup credit application readiness

Checked against official program information on 14 June 2026. Program rules can change; verify each official page again when submitting.

## What the website now establishes

- Gurukul AI is presented as an owned, AI-first software product rather than a consultancy service.
- The target users, Maharashtra education problem, product capabilities, Claude use case, cloud architecture, pilot plan, and statewide target are clearly described.
- Claims distinguish current development, planned controls, target scale, and future independent audits.
- Public Privacy, Terms, Responsible AI, Security, and Accessibility pages are available.
- The site disclaims affiliation with Anthropic, AWS, Google Cloud, or Microsoft unless a program is actually awarded.

## Claude for Startups

Official page: https://claude.com/programs/startups

- Any early-stage founder can apply to the community program.
- Credits and additional benefits currently require institutional equity funding, a company founded within the last four years, and no previous Anthropic startup credits.
- Application inputs include a Claude Console account, company email, website, and short product description.
- Claude startup credits apply to the first-party Claude API, not Claude through AWS Bedrock or Google Vertex AI.

## AWS Activate

Official page: https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/

- Current baseline eligibility includes pre-Series B status, incorporation within the last ten years, an AWS paid-tier account, and either no prior Activate credits or a request for a higher amount.
- Self-funded founders can apply directly to the Founders tier.
- The Portfolio tier requires an Organization ID from an AWS Activate Provider such as an accelerator or investor.

## Google for Startups Cloud Program

Official pages:

- https://cloud.google.com/startup/benefits
- https://cloud.google.com/startup/faq

- The Start tier is for unfunded technology startups founded within the last five years that have not previously received Google Cloud credits beyond the free trial.
- The Scale tier requires verifiable institutional equity funding through Series A, age and prior-credit limits, and public funding evidence.
- The business email, public website domain, and Google Cloud billing account email domain must match.
- The AI tier requires AI to be foundational to the product, not a bolt-on feature.
- Google may reject educational institutions, government entities, consultancies, agencies, and dev shops. Gurukul AI must be submitted as the privately owned software product company, not as a school or services agency.

## Microsoft for Startups

Official pages:

- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/microsoft-for-startups/overview
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/microsoft-for-startups/application

- The company must own a software-based product, be privately held and for-profit, operate in a supported Azure country, and remain before Series C.
- Educational institutions, government organizations, consultancies, and agencies are excluded.
- Be ready with the registered startup name, registered address, industry, website, verified personal contact email and phone, and Azure payment details.
- An Investor Network referral code can unlock higher benefits but is optional.

## Inputs still required before applying

These facts cannot be invented on the website and should match legal records and every application:

1. Registered legal company name and business address.
2. Incorporation date, company type, and privately held/for-profit status.
3. Funding stage, institutional investor details, and public funding evidence if applicable.
4. A company domain and working company-domain email. This is especially important for Google Cloud and Anthropic.
5. Claude Console, AWS paid-tier, Google Cloud billing, and Azure account identifiers.
6. Current MVP/demo URL, pilot schools or letters of intent, active-user evidence, and monthly infrastructure estimates.
7. A formally assigned privacy/grievance contact and security contact before processing live student data.

## India compliance readiness

The website reflects a privacy-by-design roadmap for child data, India-region deployment, teacher approval, no targeted advertising, role-based access, and audit logging. It does not claim completed legal compliance or certification.

Before a live school pilot, obtain legal and independent security review for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act and phased 2025 Rules, applicable CERT-In directions, school contracts and consent, content licensing, and any required GIGW/STQC government-hosting assessment.
